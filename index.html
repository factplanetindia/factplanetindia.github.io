About Fact Planet

Fact Planet is a small treasure hunt across India - real facts, simple science, farming notes, and the hidden secrets of Himalayan herbs. One herb, one photo, one secret at a time. Everything here is checked, honestly sourced, and written in plain words.

About the maker

Fact Planet is made by Piyush Sharma, a biotech researcher and curious mind from Himachal Pradesh, who completed a one-year biotechnology programme under NEP 2020. He grew up around these mountains and their herbs, and he built this site to share what they quietly teach - no tall claims, only love for real knowledge.
